# Groundhog Day

In Groundhog Day, we follow the perspective of asshole weather reporter **Phil Connors**, who ends up trapped in a time loop of the titular Groundhog Day. In these loops, Phil slowly changes as a person and even after multiple attempts to end his life, he finds purpose in making the town happy, which is what breaks the loop. Time loops are one of my favorite devices in dissecting what makes a character tick, and also a favorite way to progress their character as they're forced to confront their choices over and over again.
